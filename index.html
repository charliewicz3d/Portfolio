In development

Hollow (working title)

An exploration-only narrative game with no combat. An amnesiac protagonist wakes in a mystical forest and gradually pieces together who they are. The world is split in two: ordinary woods, and a more atmospheric spirit forest on the far side of a translucent, reflective barrier. I am building it in 3D, with a visual style inspired by What Remains of Edith Finch.

Research

Data work with real consequences

In the Duong Lab at Einstein College of Medicine, I work with large imaging datasets and vision-language models, with Python and a high-performance computing cluster.

Manuscript in preparation

Hallucination verification for chest X-ray report generation

Evaluating how vision-language models write radiology reports from chest X-rays, and checking their generated findings for hallucinations. Uses LLaVA-Med and DenseNet-121 on MIMIC-CXR-JPG, run on the Einstein HPC cluster.

  • LLaVA-Med
  • DenseNet-121
  • MIMIC-CXR-JPG
  • HPC
In progress

An independent project studying how chest X-ray findings co-occur with psychiatric diagnoses, using MIMIC-CXR and MIMIC-IV with matched controls.
